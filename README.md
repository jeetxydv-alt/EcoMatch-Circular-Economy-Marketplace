 # ♻️ EcoMatch

## AI-Powered B2B Marketplace for Circular Industrial Materials

> Turning industrial surplus into the next industry's resource.

EcoMatch is an AI-powered B2B marketplace designed to help industries discover, exchange and reuse surplus industrial materials.

The platform connects organizations that have reusable surplus materials with organizations looking for specific materials.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Large quantities of reusable industrial materials remain unused because industries lack a trusted and intelligent exchange ecosystem.

### Seller Problems

- Surplus materials may remain idle or lose value.
- Finding nearby industrial buyers is difficult.
- Generic marketplaces do not capture detailed industrial specifications.
- Material condition information can be subjective.

### Buyer Problems

- Finding exact material specifications takes time.
- Search results can be too broad.
- Price, quantity, distance and specifications must be compared manually.
- Buyers need clearer information about listing reliability.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

EcoMatch provides a two-sided industrial marketplace where:

### Sellers can

- Create material listings
- Upload product photographs
- Add quantity and specifications
- Set expected price
- Manage availability
- Connect with interested buyers

### Buyers can

- Describe their material requirement
- Set budget and quantity
- Set maximum distance
- Filter materials
- Receive AI-powered recommendations
- Compare suitable listings
- Contact sellers

---

## 🤖 AI Requirement-to-Material Matching

EcoMatch allows buyers to describe their requirements using natural language.

Example:

"I need aluminium for a door, around 20 kg, 2–3 mm thick, within 20 km and under ₹8,000."

The AI converts this requirement into structured information:

- Material: Aluminium
- Purpose: Door
- Quantity: ~20 kg
- Thickness: 2–3 mm
- Budget: ≤ ₹8,000
- Distance: ≤ 20 km

The recommendation engine then ranks suitable listings.

Example:

- Listing A → 94% match
- Listing B → 87% match

The system can also explain why a listing was recommended.

## 🏪 Key Features

### 1. Industrial Marketplace

Support for reusable industrial materials such as:

- Metals
- Plastics
- Wood
- Electrical and electronic materials
- Packaging
- Rubber
- Glass
- Construction materials

### 2. Smart Filters

Users can filter by:

- Price
- Distance
- Quantity
- Category
- Material form
- Verification status
- Category-specific specifications

### 3. Dynamic Specifications

Different material categories can have different specifications such as:

- Thickness
- Dimensions
- Grade
- Length
- Weight
- Capacity

### 4. Material Verification

EcoMatch separates seller-provided information from verified information.

Possible statuses:

- Verified
- Seller Declared
- Unverified

AI can help identify missing or inconsistent specifications.

### 5. Material Passport

Each material can have a digital identity containing:

- Unique Material ID
- Material category
- Specifications
- Quantity
- Current owner
- Listing status
- Ownership events
- Transfer history
- Lifecycle status

### 6. Carbon Impact Analytics

The platform tracks:

- Material reused
- Material diverted from disposal
- Estimated CO2e impact
- Impact by material category
- Impact over time
